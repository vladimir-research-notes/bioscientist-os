Entity & claim extraction

Done (M4)

A transparent, non-LLM baseline: dictionary entity recognition and claim-sentence extraction, every result with a confidence and an exact source span.

Brief

Extraction turns an ingested corpus into recognized entities and extracted claims. Entities are found by matching a dictionary built from the fixtures against each abstract; claims are the abstract sentences that mention at least one recognized entity, optionally with a naive subject–relation–object triple. Nothing here is a model: a reviewer can print the whole dictionary, and every result records the paper, the exact character span it was read from, the named method, a confidence, and the data source.

biosci extract --db results/raw/bioscientist.db
biosci extract --db results/raw/bioscientist.db --paper biosci-paper-006
biosci extract --db results/raw/bioscientist.db --json
python3 scripts/03_extract_claims.py   # writes results/processed/claims.{json,csv}, entities.csv

Recognized entities are observed metadata (read from the text); extracted claims are extracted claims; a triple, when filled, is part of the claim, not an inferred relation. No support/conflict judgement and no biological assertion is made here — that is the evidence table (M5, done), and even there the support/conflict/unknown label is a heuristic textual signal.

Deeper

The dictionary is built from the committed fixtures plus a small, explicit curated seed. Each UniProt protein record contributes one entity whose surface forms are its gene symbol, common name, full protein name, and accession, and whose db_xrefs are the real UniProt accession and HGNC symbol. Open Targets associations add extra surface synonyms to the protein they name (e.g. IL-6 for the IL6 protein — a form UniProt does not list but abstracts use). A short, fully-listed curated seed adds process / phenotype / chemical terms with no protein record (senescence, autophagy, inflammation, the SASP, …). On the committed corpus that is 26 entities over 61 surface forms.

Recognition is longest-match (max-munch): at each position the recognizer prefers the longest dictionary surface, so senescence-associated secretory phenotype is one SASP mention rather than three. Tokenization matches the search tokenizer (lower-cased, internal hyphens kept), so IL-6 and IL6 stay distinct tokens — the dictionary, not the tokenizer, unifies them. Extraction reads the abstract only, so every span indexes one well-defined field and abstract[start:end] reproduces the stored snippet exactly (a tested invariant: 0 of 105 round-trips fail). Entities that appear only in a title are not recognized — a documented limitation, not a hidden one.

Claims are abstract sentences containing at least one recognized entity — a deliberately high-precision, limited-recall rule that keeps each claim grounded. Relation templates are proximity based and syntax-free: when a sentence contains a relation cue with a recognized entity on each side, the nearest entity before the cue is the subject and the nearest after it is the object. Directed verbs yield rule:svo (e.g. autophagy —promotes→ cellular senescence); association cues yield rule:assoc (associated_with). This misses paraphrase and complex syntax by construction.

Confidence is a transparent additive salience score in [0, 1], not a probability: 0.35 base (grounded in an entity), +0.25 (≥2 distinct entities), +0.30 (a triple was extracted), +0.10 (a reporting cue such as “observed”/“found”). The components are named so any score can be reconstructed. Recognized entities are exact dictionary matches, so their confidence is 1.0 (this baseline does not attempt disambiguation).

ImplementationDone (M4): evidence/dictionary.py (fixture-built EntityDictionary, longest-match), evidence/text.py (span-exact tokenizer + sentence splitter), evidence/extract.py (entity/claim/relation extraction + DB loaders), and result containers in evidence/model.py, wired to biosci extract and scripts/03_extract_claims.py.
Tests / results24 extraction tests (143 total, offline). On the 18 ingested abstracts: 26 dictionary entities / 61 surfaces, 63 entity mentions, 42 claims, 15 with a relation triple; all 105 spans round-trip against the abstract. The designed conflict pair surfaces (autophagy promotes vs restrains senescence). These are extraction counts, not quality metrics — precision/recall against gold labels is Milestone 8.
Open questionsWhether subject/object should be the canonical entity name (current) or the verbatim surface; how much recall the abstract-only, entity-grounded rule sacrifices; whether to add light morphology to the relation cues without losing auditability.
