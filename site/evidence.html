Evidence tables

Done (M5)

The core artifact: claims grouped by a research question, linked to papers, entities, and databases, with visible per-cell provenance.

Brief

An evidence table is built for a research question. Each row is one extracted claim that mentions a question entity, carrying its paper, exact source snippet and span, recognized entity names, database links, a heuristic support/conflict/unknown signal, the claim's extraction method, a confidence, and a notes field that records how the support label and links were derived.

biosci evidence \
  --question "What evidence links cellular senescence to chronic inflammation?" \
  --db results/raw/bioscientist.db --out results/processed/evidence_table.csv

The entities and claims that fill these rows come from Milestone 4 (see Extraction): dictionary-recognized entities with db_xrefs and exact spans, and entity-grounded claim sentences with optional relation triples and a confidence. Milestone 5 groups them by a research question and adds the support/conflict/unknown signal — a heuristic textual signal, never a biological verdict (safety).

How a row is built

  1. Question entities. The question text and its entities_of_interest are run through the same entity dictionary, so a curated fixture question or a free-text question both resolve to recognized entity ids. Terms with no fixture record are reported as unmatched, not hidden.
  2. Relevance. A claim is kept iff it mentions ≥ 1 of those entities — a transparent, grounded filter.
  3. Support / conflict / unknown. A transparent cue heuristic (rule:support-cues): a strong negation cue (e.g. not associated) → conflict; an asserted relationship (a relation triple or a support cue) → support; otherwise → unknown (the honest default). The cue that fired is written into notes.
  4. Database links. UniProt/HGNC cross-references for the claim's entities plus any Open Targets-style association records naming their gene — an inferred relation, labeled as such.

The four epistemic levels stay separate within one row: the snippet is an extracted claim, entity_names are observed metadata, database_links are inferred relations, and the support label is a heuristic textual signal. The row's provenance is anchored to the claim snippet, so provenance coverage is 100% by construction.

Columns

claim_id, paper_id, claim_text, entity_names, support_conflict_unknown, confidence, extraction_method, source_snippet, char_start, char_end, database_links, notes, data_source, epistemic_level — the columns docs/PLAN.md requires, plus the exact span, data source, and epistemic level.

ImplementationDone (M5). evidence/table.py (builder, support heuristic, question resolution, EvidenceReport), the biosci evidence command, and scripts/04_build_evidence_table.py.
Tests / results24 new tests (143 total, offline). On the three fixture questions: 31 / 29 / 8 rows, all at 100% provenance coverage; all three labels exercised (e.g. senescence–inflammation: 17 support, 6 conflict, 8 unknown). Counts are behavior, not quality (eval is M8).
Open questionsThe per-row label reads one sentence's phrasing; it does not detect when two papers conflict only in direction (e.g. autophagy promotes vs. restrains senescence) — both rows read as support. Cross-paper conflict detection is future work.
