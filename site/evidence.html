Evidence tables

Planned

The core artifact: claims linked to papers, entities, and databases, with visible provenance.

Brief

An evidence table is built for a research question. Each row links a claim to its paper, source snippet, entities, database links, a support/conflict/unknown signal, the extraction method, and a confidence value.

biosci evidence \
  --question "What evidence links cellular senescence to chronic inflammation?" \
  --db results/raw/bioscientist.db --out results/processed/evidence_table.csv

The entities and claims that fill these rows already exist (Milestone 4, done — see Extraction): dictionary-recognized entities with db_xrefs and exact spans, and entity-grounded claim sentences with optional relation triples and a confidence. Milestone 5 groups them by a research question and adds the support/conflict/unknown signal — a heuristic textual signal, never a biological verdict (safety).

Columns

claim_id, claim_text, entity_names, paper_id, source_snippet, support_conflict_unknown, extraction_method, confidence, database_links, notes.

ImplementationEntity/claim extraction done (M4) in evidence/ (see Extraction); the research-question-driven table builder and the support/conflict/unknown signal are Milestone 5.
Tests / resultsM4 produces 63 entity mentions and 42 claims (15 with relation triples) over the 18 abstracts. Provenance-coverage and column-completeness tests are added with the builder (M5).
Open questionsHow to represent conflict when two claims disagree only in degree, not direction.
