Ingestion

Planned

Load small fixtures into a deterministic local database.

Brief

Fixture ingestion normalizes papers, authors, journals, dates, abstracts, keywords, URLs, and database IDs, then writes them to SQLite. Re-ingest is idempotent; database creation is deterministic.

biosci ingest data/fixtures --db results/raw/bioscientist.db
biosci stats   --db results/raw/bioscientist.db
