From prototype to a research paper

What this prototype aims at is methodological and infrastructural: a reproducible, auditable pipeline whose every output traces back to a specific paper, snippet, and extraction method, and which never collapses the four epistemic levels. That is a platform, not yet a result. A credible empirical paper would need, at least:

  • larger, properly-licensed corpora and richer, multi-annotator gold labels;
  • gold support/conflict annotations so the evidence table's support label can be scored against a human verdict — today only the table's structural quality (completeness, provenance coverage) is measured;
  • ontology-grounded entity linking that preserves auditability (no opaque steps);
  • a controlled comparison of transparent baselines vs. an optional LLM adapter on provenance fidelity, not just extraction recall;
  • the core payoff — a user study of which provenance fields actually change a scientist's trust or decision.

These are honest directions, not promises. The repository remains a platform/prototype unless and until a novel empirical result is demonstrated; a candid written assessment of the publishable path — what is established, what is deliberately missing, and the threats to validity — is in docs/PAPER_PATH.md.

Non-goals (unchanged)

No clinical decision support, no diagnosis/treatment claims, no production knowledge graph, no benchmark leaderboard. See Safety and Limitations.
