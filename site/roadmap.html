Roadmap

Scaffold

From a provenance-first prototype toward a possibly publishable methodology.

Near term (the 13 milestones)

Schemas → ingestion → search → extraction → evidence tables → connectors → visualizations → evaluation → memo → optional LLM adapter → polish → cleanup. See Milestones.

Later (research directions)

  • Larger, properly-licensed corpora and richer gold labels.
  • Stronger entity linking (ontology grounding) while preserving auditability.
  • Comparing transparent baselines vs. an LLM adapter on provenance fidelity.
  • A study of which provenance fields actually change scientists' trust/decisions.

These are honest directions. The current repo is a platform/prototype unless and until a novel empirical result is demonstrated — see docs/PAPER_PATH.md (added at Milestone 12).
