Data model

Implemented (M1)

Typed pydantic v2 schemas with explicit provenance and epistemic typing.

Brief

Core schemas: Paper, Author, BiomedicalEntity, Claim, EvidenceItem, DatabaseRecord, EvidenceTable, ResearchQuestion, WorkbenchRun — plus a shared Provenance submodel and four controlled vocabularies (DataSource, EpistemicLevel, EntityType, SupportLabel). Implemented in Milestone 1.

Objects split into two families. Observed-metadata objects (Paper, DatabaseRecord) are the source and carry data_source + synthetic directly. Derived objects (BiomedicalEntity, Claim, EvidenceItem) carry a required Provenance block pointing back at a paper, snippet, and method — enforced by the type system, not by convention.

Provenance and epistemic typing

  • data_source ∈ {fixture, cache, live} on every externally sourced object; the loader stamps fixture on load.
  • synthetic: bool marks fabricated fixture records.
  • extraction_method (on Provenance) names the exact rule/model that produced a claim/entity.
  • EpistemicLevel ∈ {observed metadata, extracted claim, inferred relation, unsupported hypothesis} — asserted at import to match the CLI's four levels.
  • support_conflict_unknown is a heuristic textual signal, not a biological verdict.
  • Every model uses extra="forbid"; confidence ∈ [0,1]; char_span must be non-negative and ordered.
