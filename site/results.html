Brief

This page mirrors docs/RESULTS.md. The full pipeline has landed, so every milestone that produces measurable output is reported below: ingestion (M2), search + topic grouping (M3), entity/claim extraction (M4), evidence-table assembly (M5), the database-connector layer (M6), the figures (M7), the gold-label self-evaluation (M8), the research memo (M9), and the optional, cost-guarded LLM adapter (M10, off the default path). Every rate carries its denominator; small N means the intervals are wide on purpose. These measure pipeline behaviour on a tiny, partly-synthetic corpus — not population performance or biological truth.

Headline self-evaluation (M8)

Produced by biosci eval against committed gold labels; 95% Wilson intervals. The dictionary recognizer is high precision by construction and loses on recall (both misses documented in data/fixtures/gold/README.md); claim sanity, completeness, and provenance are 1.0 by construction and act as regression floors, not achievements.
