Safety / non-clinical use

Scaffold

A research-assistance tool. NOT a medical-advice, diagnostic, or treatment tool.

Hard constraints

bioscientist-os must not present output as medical advice, provide patient-specific clinical decision support, assert drug efficacy/safety/dosing, or imply that an extracted/inferred relation is a clinical fact. Any output that could read as clinical advice is a defect.

Epistemic honesty

Every summary or memo separates and visibly labels four levels: observed metadata, extracted claim, inferred relation, and unsupported hypothesis. "Support / conflict" describes textual agreement between claims, not biological truth. Suggested experiments are hypotheses for humans to evaluate, never protocols or recommendations.

ImplementationSafety statement is part of the contract from the first commit.
Tests / resultsMemo generation (M9) must pass the non-clinical labeling rules.
Open questionsContinuous: ensure no generated text drifts toward clinical phrasing.
