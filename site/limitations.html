Limitations

Scaffold

A prototype optimized for auditability and provenance, not coverage or sophistication.

Brief

The corpus is tiny and partly synthetic; extraction is deliberately simple and transparent (low recall); support/conflict signals describe text, not biology. Read every result with the denominators in Results.

Possible misleading artifacts

  • Clean-looking evidence tables can imply more certainty than warranted — read the confidence, extraction_method, and support_conflict_unknown columns.
  • Synthetic fixtures look like real papers; they are marked synthetic: true.
  • A high point estimate at small N is not a strong result.
  • Visualizations summarize a tiny corpus and are not field maps.
