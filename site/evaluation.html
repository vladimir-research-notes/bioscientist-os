Evaluation

Done

Small but rigorous self-evaluation with explicit denominators and Wilson confidence intervals.

Brief

Evaluates the pipeline, not biological truth: ingestion correctness, BM25F search recall on labeled queries, entity precision/recall against tiny gold labels, claim-extraction sanity, evidence-table completeness, and provenance coverage. Every rate is a count k/n that carries its denominator and a 95% Wilson interval; heterogeneous tasks are never collapsed into one headline number, and small N is stated honestly.

biosci eval --db results/raw/bioscientist.db --out results/processed/eval_report.json
python3 scripts/06_run_eval.py
python3 scripts/06_run_eval.py --confidence 0.9 --recall-k 1 --recall-k 5

Measured on the fixtures + gold labels

Gold labels are committed under data/fixtures/gold/ (6 queries / 16 relevant pairs; 6 abstracts / 24 gold entities) and were authored by reading the corpus, independently of the ranker. These are pipeline numbers on a tiny, partly-synthetic corpus — not biological truth.

Honest reading: small N → wide intervals (a perfect 40/40 still has a lower bound of 0.912). The dictionary recognizer is high-precision by construction (no fuzzy matching → no false positives here) and loses on recall: the two misses are documented — IL-8 has no fixture record, and p16INK4a-expressing is one hyphenated token so the p16INK4a surface does not match. Search recall@3 < 1.0 because two relevant papers rank just outside the top 3 and are recovered by k=5. Claim sanity, completeness, and provenance are 1.0 by construction — regression floors, not achievements.

ImplementationDone (Milestone 8): the evals package — metrics.py (the Proportion + Wilson interval, critical value from the stdlib statistics.NormalDist), gold.py (typed loaders for the committed gold labels), and harness.py (six evaluators, the EvalReport container, and the database-backed run_eval(db)) — plus the biosci eval CLI command and scripts/06_run_eval.py.
Tests / results28 evaluation tests (223 total): the Wilson math and its edge cases, the gold loader with a modeled-flag cross-check against the live dictionary, each evaluator against its committed denominators (including the two documented entity misses), run_eval determinism, and the biosci eval CLI. The report (eval_report.json) is byte-identical across runs.
Open questionsN is small and gold labels are single-annotator; intervals are wide and convey uncertainty, not certification. The support/conflict label is not yet scored against a human verdict (M8 measures structural quality only) — see roadmap.
