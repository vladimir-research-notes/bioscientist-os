Evaluation

Planned

Small but rigorous self-evaluation with explicit denominators and confidence intervals.

Brief

Evaluates the pipeline, not biological truth: ingestion correctness, search recall on labeled queries, entity precision/recall against tiny gold labels, claim sanity, evidence completeness, and provenance coverage. Every metric reports its denominator; proportions report a 95% Wilson interval.

biosci eval --db results/raw/bioscientist.db --out results/processed/eval_report.json
